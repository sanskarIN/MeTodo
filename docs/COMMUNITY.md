## Community Vision

### Our Mission

MeTodo is an open-source task management application built by and for the community. Our mission is to create a powerful, user-friendly, and privacy-respecting tool that helps people organize their lives and achieve their goals.

### Our Values

**1. Open Source**
- Source code is freely available
- Community can inspect and contribute
- Transparent development process
- No proprietary restrictions

**2. User Privacy**
- Data stays on user's device
- No tracking or profiling
- Transparent data practices
- User control over data

**3. Quality**
- High code quality standards
- Comprehensive testing
- Professional documentation
- Performance optimization

**4. Accessibility**
- Inclusive design
- Accessible to all users
- Multiple languages (future)
- Diverse perspectives welcome

**5. Community-Driven**
- Community shapes the roadmap
- User feedback valued
- Collaborative development
- Shared ownership

## Code of Conduct

### Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or socioeconomic status.

### Expected Behavior

**Be Respectful**
- Treat all community members with respect
- Listen to different perspectives
- Accept constructive criticism
- Disagree respectfully

**Be Inclusive**
- Welcome newcomers
- Help others learn
- Include diverse perspectives
- Celebrate differences

**Be Collaborative**
- Work together toward common goals
- Share knowledge and experience
- Support fellow contributors
- Build each other up

**Be Professional**
- Maintain professional communication
- Avoid personal attacks
- Stay focused on issues
- Keep discussions on topic

**Be Honest**
- Be truthful in communications
- Admit mistakes
- Give credit where due
- Avoid deception

### Unacceptable Behavior

**Harassment**
- Offensive comments
- Intimidation
- Threats
- Unwanted attention

**Discrimination**
- Discriminatory language
- Exclusionary behavior
- Prejudiced comments
- Biased treatment

**Trolling**
- Deliberately provocative posts
- Off-topic disruptions
- Spam
- Bad faith arguments

**Abuse**
- Personal attacks
- Insults
- Derogatory language
- Hostile behavior

**Misconduct**
- Plagiarism
- Unauthorized use of others' work
- Dishonest representation
- Fraud

### Enforcement

**Reporting Violations**

If you witness or experience a violation of this code of conduct, please report it:

- **Email:** supportramsandesh@gmail.com
- **GitHub:** Report to maintainers
- **Subject:** "Code of Conduct Violation"

**Investigation Process**

1. Report is received and reviewed
2. Investigation is conducted
3. Appropriate action is taken
4. Outcome is communicated

**Consequences**

Violations may result in:
- Warning
- Temporary suspension
- Permanent removal
- Legal action (if applicable)

**Take Action**
- Address feedback

- Learn from experience

- Continue improving

## Dispute Resolution

### Conflict Resolution Process

**Step 1: Direct Communication**
- Discuss the issue privately
- Listen to both perspectives
- Try to find common ground
- Document the conversation

**Step 2: Mediation**
- Involve a neutral third party
- Facilitate discussion
- Help find solutions
- Document the process

**Step 3: Escalation**
- Involve project maintainers
- Review the situation
- Make a decision
- Communicate outcome

**Step 4: Appeal**
- Request review of decision
- Provide additional information
- Involve higher authority
- Final decision

### Conflict Types

**Technical Disagreements**
- Different approaches
- Implementation debates
- Architecture discussions
- Solution differences

**Interpersonal Conflicts**
- Personality clashes
- Communication issues
- Respect problems
- Behavior concerns

**Governance Issues**
- Decision-making
- Process disagreements
- Policy conflicts
- Authority questions

### Resolution Principles

**Fairness**
- Treat all fairly
- Consider all perspectives
- Make unbiased decisions
- Document reasoning

**Transparency**
- Communicate openly
- Explain decisions
- Share information
- Be honest

**Respect**
- Respect all parties
- Value perspectives
- Maintain dignity
- Preserve relationships

**Efficiency**
- Resolve quickly
- Avoid prolonged conflict
- Focus on solutions
- Move forward
