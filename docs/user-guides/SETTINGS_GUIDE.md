## Introduction

### What Are Settings?

Settings are preferences that control how MeTodo behaves and appears. Customize the app to match your needs and workflow.

### Why Customize Settings?

- **Personalization:** Make app feel like yours
- **Productivity:** Optimize for your workflow
- **Accessibility:** Adapt to your needs
- **Privacy:** Control your data
- **Notifications:** Manage alerts

## Troubleshooting

### Settings Not Saving

**Problem:** Changes don't save
**Solution:**
1. Check internet connection
2. Restart app
3. Try again
4. Contact support if persists

### Settings Reset

**Problem:** Settings reverted
**Solution:**
1. Check auto-sync
2. Restore from backup
3. Manually reconfigure
4. Contact support

### Performance Issues

**Problem:** App slow after settings change
**Solution:**
1. Enable "Reduce Animations"
2. Enable "Memory Optimization"
3. Clear cache
4. Restart app
5. Revert settings if needed
