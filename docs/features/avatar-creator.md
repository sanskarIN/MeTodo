# Avatar Creator Guide

## Overview

The Avatar Creator allows you to design a unique avatar that represents you in MeTodo. Your custom avatar appears on the home screen and throughout the app, making your experience more personal and engaging.

## Creating Your Avatar

### Step-by-Step Guide

1. Navigate to the **Avatar** tab
2. You'll see a preview of your avatar in the center
3. Customize each feature using the options below
4. Changes update in real-time
5. Tap **Save Avatar** when satisfied

## Saving Your Avatar

### Save Process

1. Customize all desired features
2. Review the preview
3. Tap **Save Avatar**
4. Avatar is stored locally on your device
5. Avatar appears on home screen immediately

### Editing Your Avatar

To modify your avatar later:

1. Go to the **Avatar** tab
2. Your current avatar settings load
3. Make desired changes
4. Tap **Save Avatar** to update

## Avatar Display

Your avatar appears in:

- **Home Screen** - Top right corner header
- **Profile Section** - User identification
- **Settings** - Avatar preview
- **Task Completion** - Achievement display

## Storage & Privacy

- Avatars are stored **locally on your device**
- No data is sent to external servers
- Your avatar is never shared without permission
- Complete privacy and control over your data

## Troubleshooting

### Avatar Not Saving

- Ensure all selections are complete
- Check device storage space
- Try saving again
- Restart the app if needed

### Avatar Not Displaying

- Refresh the home screen
- Check avatar tab to verify it was saved
- Restart the app
- Clear app cache in Settings

### Preview Not Updating

- Ensure you've made a selection
- Wait a moment for preview to update
- Try scrolling the customization options
- Restart the app

## Advanced Tips

### Creating Themed Avatars

Create multiple avatars by:
1. Customizing your current avatar
2. Noting the color combinations
3. Saving as your main avatar
4. Using the same process to create variations
